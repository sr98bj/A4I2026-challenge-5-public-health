🛡️ Multi-Agent Context Separation (Why We Advance)

A single agent or rubber-stamp auditor will naturally park at shopping centers in affluent Buckhead because parking is large and easy. Our system prevents this through structural separation:

Agent 1 (Planner): Sees ONLY physical turning radius, parking stalls, and pavement. Programmatically forbidden from seeing income or uninsured rates.
Agent 2 (Auditor): Holds the restricted tract_access dataset. Detects the "Convenience Trap" and rejects sites with low healthcare burden.
Agent 3 (Communicator): Translates the audited stops into plain-English flyers mathematically verified at a 6th-grade reading level.
